- PDF.js (Apache-2.0) https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js
- Tesseract.js (Apache-2.0) https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- PaddleOCR.js / PP-OCRv6 models (Apache-2.0) https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
- ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
- OpenCV.js via @techstark/opencv-js (Apache-2.0) https://opencv.org
- clipper-lib (Boost / BSL-1.0)
- js-yaml (MIT)

Documents you open in this app are processed only in your browser and are not sent to a server.
